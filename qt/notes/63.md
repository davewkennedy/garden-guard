## Plan

### A) Wildcard bats (all 🦇 Bats)
- **Movement:** free flight instead of a sky lane. Enemy gets `heading`, `spawnTime`, `startDist`, and a short `trail` (about 10 points sampled every 0.08 s).
  - Steering: each frame the heading turns toward a blend of a *wander direction* and the *direction to the house*.
    - The wander direction comes from a sum of 2–3 sines with per-bat phases and frequencies hashed from `e.id`. It's smooth and deterministic, so the simtest stays stable.
    - House pull weight w = clamp(age / 18 s, 0, 1)². At age 18 s or more it flies straight home.
    - Edge avoidance: within 1 tile of the board edge, blend in the inward normal.
    - The turn rate is capped (about 2.5 rad/s), so it reads as smooth arcs, not jitter.
  - Arrival: within 0.6 tile of the house = leak (1 life). Hard cap at age 30 s: it's pulled straight in, so it always arrives in bounded time (~25 s typical).
  - `e.dist` = startDistToHouse − currentDistToHouse (≥ 0), so "first" targeting still prefers the bat closest to home.
- **Stats:** Bat reward → 30 (a catch bonus), lives 1. hp/speed unchanged.
- **Tells:** glowing red eyes plus a faint fading trail in drawEnemies. On spawn: `bugSound("bug_screech")` and the new signal `wildcard(int enemyId)` for the lead's toast.
- Butterflies keep sky lanes.
