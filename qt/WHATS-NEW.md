# What's new in Garden Guard++ 🌱

*Compared with Garden Guard+.*

## 🗺️ Campaign

- **10 maps instead of 5**, still 5 waves each, with 20 lives for the whole run.
- **Growing gardens:** boards grow from 15×10 tiles (Sunny Meadow) to 30×20 (Evergreen Summit), with longer paths and bigger swarms. Start coins rise to match, from 180 up to 960–1100.
- **Seasons:** the campaign travels from 🌸 spring (maps 1–2) through ☀️ summer (3–5) and 🍂 autumn (6–7) to ❄️ winter (8–10). Each season has its own look and weather: drifting petals, bright flowers, tumbling leaves, falling snow.
- **Plant unlocks:** you start with 4 plants. The rest arrive map by map: 🌽🍇 on map 2, 🌵🍄 on map 3, and 🌶️🌹🍀 on map 4. *Play again* starts back at map 1 with the first 4.

## 🐛 New plants & bugs

- **🍇 Grape Vine (new):** lassos a flyer and pulls it to the ground for 2.5–3.5 s, where even 🎃 mortars and 🌶️ chilis can hit it.
- **🌵 Cactus Sniper, new role:** it now deals **+50% damage to scorpions** and outranges their goo. It's your anti-scorpion plant.
- **🦇 Wildcard bats:** bats no longer follow a sky lane. They flap their own unpredictable route (red eyes, purple trail), cost 1 life, and pay a hefty **30 coins**.

## 🌍 New mechanics

- **Terrain:** 🪨 boulders and ponds block planting, but you *can* plant on 🪷 lily pads. On winter maps the ponds are frozen.
- **❄️ Deep snow (maps 8–10):** ground bugs move at 85% speed, but Frost Ferns are a little weaker. Flyers aren't slowed.
- **🌬️ Wind gusts (maps 6–7):** every ~12 s a gust (with a warning toast and a whoosh) pushes airborne flyers up to 1.5 tiles off their lane. Grounded flyers and ground bugs don't budge.
- **Two entrances:** on Pumpkin Patch a second path enters from the top and crosses the main path three times, so bugs come from two nests.

## 🎮 New controls

- **🎯 Focus fire:** select a plant, or **double-click** to select every plant of that type, then **click a bug**. They all gang up on it until it dies or escapes. `Esc` cancels.
- **Upgrade all:** with a group selected, one button upgrades every plant in it.
- **📖 Garden Journal (`J`):** every bug you've met and every plant you've unlocked, with stats and counter tips. Unmet entries show as `???` teasers.
- **🗺️ Choose map:** from the intro card, replay any garden you've reached with full lives and its plants unlocked, and see your best stars for each.
- Shop keys now run `1`–`9`, `0` and `-` for all 11 plants.

## ✨ Quality

- **Readable big maps:** on large boards, plants, bugs, HP bars, coin popups, targeting markers and the boss bar are drawn larger.
- **One combined card:** "New bugs… and new plants to stop them!" introduces each map's new bugs, its new plants, and its features (night, wind, snow, ponds, boulders, two entrances).
- **NEW ribbons** mark freshly unlocked plants in the shop.
- Winter sky lanes are blue so they stay visible on snow.

## 🌟 Try this first

1. On **map 2**, plant a 🍇 Grape Vine under the sky lane, watch it lasso a butterfly, then let a 🎃 mortar finish it.
2. **Double-click** a sprout to select them all, then click the lead beetle and watch them focus fire.
3. Press **`J`** after each new map and see the journal fill in.
4. On **Amber Woods**, watch a gust push the butterflies off their lane, and plan anti-air with a little slack.
5. Reach **Evergreen Summit** (30×20, three snails, deep snow) and go for ★★★. Use **Choose map** to retry it with full lives.
