# Garden Guard++ web: real-phone test checklist

We tested everything we could in an emulated browser (iPhone, Android and iPad sizes, touch, pinch).
These are the things only a real phone can confirm. It takes about 10 minutes per phone.
If anything fails, note the phone model, the browser, and what you saw.

## Setup
1. Host the files: upload the contents of `dist/gardenguard-web/` (or unzip `dist/gardenguard-web.zip`)
   to your host. It must be **https**.
2. Open the address on the phone.

## iPhone (Safari)
- [ ] Held upright, it says "Turn your phone sideways". Sideways, the game appears.
- [ ] Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. The 🌻 icon appears on the home screen.
- [ ] Open it from the icon. It runs **full screen** with no Safari bars.
- [ ] **Sound**: tap "Let's grow!" and plant something. You should hear it. (The ringer/silent
      switch can mute web sounds on iPhone. Try with the switch off silent if it's quiet.)
- [ ] Leave the app (home screen), come back, tap: the sound still works.
- [ ] Pinch to zoom in on the garden, drag to move around, then tap 🔍 to zoom back out.
      The whole page should NOT zoom; only the garden does.
- [ ] Pick a plant, then press and hold on the grass: a magnifier bubble shows where it will go.
      Slide your finger and lift: it's planted there.
- [ ] Double-tap a plant: all plants of that kind are selected. Tap a bug: they focus fire on it.
- [ ] ▸ tab on the side panel hides/shows the panel.

## Android (Chrome)
- [ ] The full-screen button (four corners, top right) hides the browser bars and keeps it sideways.
- [ ] The intro card shows "📲 Install as an app". Install it, then open it from the icon:
      full screen.
- [ ] Sound plays after the first tap.
- [ ] Same pinch, magnifier, double-tap and ▸ checks as on the iPhone.

## Both phones
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode, then open the game from its icon: it still loads and plays (offline cache).
- [ ] Play into map 2, then fully close the app (swipe it away). Reopen it: the intro offers
      "▶ Resume your run" with the right map and lives.
- [ ] On a big map (8, 9 or 10, via 🗺️ Choose map): picking a plant and tapping the garden first
      zooms in ("tap again to plant"). Then a second tap plants it.
- [ ] 🤖 Autopilot plays smoothly at 2× and 3× (no stutter). Phones that get warm or stutter: tell us.
- [ ] Nothing important is cut off at the edges, including next to the notch or camera hole.
